02 / Lovable + Gemini Nano Banana Pro

PersonaFit website + mockups

I used Lovable to build a shopping website prototype based on competitive research, then Gemini to create mobile mockups. The idea was to let someone browse clothes and check the fit using a virtual version of themselves.

03 / Google AI Studio

Content studio + focus website

I used Google AI Studio to try out two website ideas: a content studio that follows a brand guide, and a focus page with a timer and media. I worked through changes to the layout and features based on what each page needed to do.

Brand-guided contentWeb prototypingInterface refinement

I built a custom ChatGPT assistant with uploaded reference documents and instructions for a specific topic. I also used IdeaFloat for competitive research and to explore TAM, SAM, and SOM—the overall market, the part a business could serve, and the part it could realistically reach.
